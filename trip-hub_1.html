Heathrow at 05:30 on a Saturday

A 07:30 departure means bag drop done by 05:30 — leaving home around 03:30 with four people and eight cases. A night at the Sofitel, which is connected to Terminal 2, turns that into a 05:00 start. Costed in the workbook.

Frankfurt is a 90-minute connection

10:05 in, 11:35 out, both Terminal 1, bags checked through to Tokyo. It's legal but there is no slack, and you pass Schengen exit control on the way. If LH923 runs late, this is the point of failure — know the later Frankfurt–Tokyo services before you fly.

The 08:35 out of Haneda — pre-book a taxi

ANA's check-in counters at Terminal 3 don't open until 06:25, and the cut-off is 07:35. So arrive at 06:30 and no earlier: a 05:00 alarm, a taxi at 05:30, at the terminal by 06:10. Book a jumbo taxi — four adults with eight checked bags won't fit a standard Tokyo cab, and advance booking is required for the fixed fare anyway. Chiyoda-ku to Haneda is ¥14,700 including tolls, plus a ¥3,500 late-night premium before 05:59.

Leg one · Japan

Tokyo — three nights, two and a half usable days

You land at 08:45 on Sunday and leave before dawn on Wednesday, so the arrival morning has to count. Each day below stays inside one part of the city rather than criss-crossing it. Expect 12°C, bone-dry air, brilliant sunshine — and sunset at 16:30, which is why the illuminations do so much work here.

One base for all three nights

Hotel Metropolitan Edmont in Iidabashi is the front-runner — good value, five train lines, and Kagurazaka on the doorstep for dinner. Book the East Wing Twin (28 sqm) or Edmont Superior Twin (26 sqm), never the 18 sqm Edmont Standard, which is the room behind most of its bad reviews. Full shortlist and alternatives are in the project folder.

Mid-December is the cheap end of an expensive month

Japan's domestic peak, which lifts rates 40–70%, starts around the 27th for New Year. The 13th to the 16th is an ordinary Sunday-to-Wednesday. You're booking at the right time.

Leg two · Australia

New South Wales — recover, drive, then Christmas

Thirteen nights based at your brother's in Pymble, in three parts: a recovery day on the 17th, then five nights on the road — coast, vineyards, mountains — while his family are still at work, and from the 23rd Christmas in Pymble with trips into the city by train. Expect 25–27°C, humidity, the odd afternoon thunderstorm, and daylight until eight.

Two things follow from leaving on the 18th

Change the car hire. It's booked 17–23 Dec; move it to 18–23 — five days not six, saving about A$130, and rebooking is free. And leave early. NSW school holidays start that same Friday, so you'll be driving out with the whole of Sydney rather than a day ahead of it. On the road by eight. It also makes the Shoal Bay booking more urgent, not less — that week is the hardest bed to get in New South Wales.

Isaac can't drive; Eva can, for a fee

Almost every Australian rental company sets a minimum age of 21, so at 18 Isaac is out (Bayswater/No Birds is the one exception, at A$20/day). Eva at 21 falls in the young-driver band — A$9.90/day with East Coast up to A$27.50 with Thrifty. Plan on you and Abigail sharing the 650 km.

The Fingal Spit crossing — please read this oneThe sand spit out to Fingal Island looks like an easy walk. Seventeen people have died attempting it.
